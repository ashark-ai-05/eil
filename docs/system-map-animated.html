Why the vector packet detours

It probes IVF clusters first, ranks survivors by Hamming distance on sig, then rescores only those exactly. Measured 298.5 µs → 1.30 µs per chunk and 30 MB → 1035 kB of storage. The detour is the whole scale story.

nprobe, not oversample

Measured: 8× and 16× oversample are identical at every nprobe. Once survivors are rescored exactly, binary quantization costs nothing — all loss is clusters not probed. So oversample is fixed and nprobe is an output of calibration with a ≥0.98 gate, never a constant in the code.

What already shipped

audit_log now carries duration_ms · ok · error · route · executor · trace_id, failures are audited rather than silently dropped, and retrieval_events records query → ranked ids. That is step 0 — and it is what produces the real queries the eval harness replays.

The one thing not drawn

Evaluation. It has no packet because it is not on the request path — it is the gate around it. Recall@50 and Recall@10 as a pair, nDCG@10, paired permutation test. Nothing downstream of it merges at p ≥ 0.05.
